# Engineer Karl — Application Overview

Engineer Karl supports The Gaming Council's in-game guilds and Discord community. It handles recruitment reporting, staff coordination, member records, contribution tracking, PTO, support tickets, and moderation evidence.

Staff use whispers to recruit players into the guilds, then ROs (reach-outs) to help those recruits participate in the community. Nightly activity leaderboards and memberflow reports help leadership assign work and assess results.

## Requested access

- **Guild Members:** Membership events and role populations for reports, staff eligibility, acknowledgments, records, and service access.
- **Message Content:** Bridged recruitment chatter, announcement text, message records, archives, ticket discussions, attachments, and security review of text-command attempts.

Command receipts store who invoked which command, when, and where. They do not store arguments, message bodies, attachments, or interaction tokens.
